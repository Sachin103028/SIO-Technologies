Refund Policy

Our commitment to fair, transparent refunds — so you can work with us with complete confidence.

Last updated: June 2026

At SIO Technologies, we stand behind the quality of our work. This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for our services. By engaging our services, you agree to the terms outlined below.

1. Project Deposits

All projects require an upfront deposit before work begins. This deposit covers initial discovery, planning, and resource allocation specific to your project.

  • Refundable within 48 hours: If you cancel before any work has started, your deposit will be refunded in full within 7 business days.
  • Non-refundable after work begins: Once discovery, design, or development work has commenced, the deposit is non-refundable as it compensates for time and resources already invested.

2. Milestone-Based Projects

For projects structured around milestones:

  • Payments made for a completed milestone are non-refundable.
  • If you cancel mid-project, you are liable only for milestones completed or actively in progress at the time of cancellation.
  • Any advance payment for future milestones not yet started will be refunded within 7 business days of the cancellation request.

3. Monthly Retainer Services

For ongoing monthly services (e.g. maintenance, support, hosting management):

  • You may cancel at any time with 14 days' written notice.
  • Fees for the current billing period are non-refundable once the period has begun.
  • Unused portions of prepaid periods beyond the current month will be refunded on a pro-rata basis.

4. Satisfaction Guarantee

We are committed to delivering work that meets the agreed scope and quality standards. If you are unsatisfied with a deliverable:

  • Raise the concern within 7 days of delivery and we will revise the work at no additional charge.
  • If after two revision rounds the work still does not meet the agreed specification, we will discuss a partial or full refund on a case-by-case basis.
  • Refund requests must be accompanied by specific, documented reasons tied to the agreed project scope.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The project was completed and delivered as per the agreed specification
  • Delays or issues caused by the client (e.g. late feedback, missing content, unresponsiveness)
  • Change of mind after work has been completed
  • Requests made more than 30 days after delivery
  • Third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (e.g. domain registration, software licences, API fees)

6. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund request, please contact us at hello@siotechnologies.online with the following details:

  • Your full name and project name
  • Date of payment and amount
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Any supporting documentation

We will review your request and respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

7. Disputes

We aim to resolve all refund requests amicably and fairly. If a dispute cannot be resolved through direct communication, both parties agree to seek mediation before pursuing any legal action. This policy is governed by the laws of India.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
